Market access for small businesses

CPS Advertisement helps eligible freelancers and small businesses test an offer in the market without paying advertising costs upfront. Promotion is backed by traceable funding. A disclosed commission is due only after an attributed sale is verified and final.

What is promised: a funded, measurable minimum test for campaigns that meet published requirements. A campaign that proves it can support more distribution may receive additional funded tranches. The idea is not infinite guaranteed impressions; it is no arbitrary reach ceiling for successful, supportable campaigns.
01 / the flow

From offer to evidence

A seller starts with a real offer and a clear limit. The platform makes the first promise only after the funding exists and tells the seller exactly what will be measured.

01

Qualify the seller

Check the rolling 12-month gross revenue ceiling of €100,000, connected businesses where relevant, and evidence for a lawful, deliverable offer.

02

Secure the funding

Settled compute proceeds, received sponsorship, or other approved reserves can back exposure. Pending compute value is not spendable.

03

Agree the terms

Show the minimum exposure, source and amount of funding, commission cap, tracking method, return reserve, and stop conditions before activation.

04

Deliver and measure

Route the campaign through permitted advertising, publisher, creator, or affiliate partners. Record promised and delivered exposure with source evidence.

05

Validate each sale

Reconcile unique order IDs and signed events. Handle refunds, returns, chargebacks, and fraud review before a sale becomes commissionable.

06

Settle and scale

When a sale is final, distribute the agreed commission. Reinvest eligible proceeds and add funding only when supply, risk, and economics support it.

02 / two connected modules

Funding and promotion belong together

The compute idea helps finance a market test, but useful computing power has no value to the fund until verified work is completed and proceeds settle.

Compute Funding Module

Voluntary contribution

Supporters can contribute idle compute through an approved provider or contracted buyer. They choose where settled value goes, set limits, and can pause or leave. Sellers do not need to contribute their own device capacity.

03 / operating principles

Rules that protect trust

Open, explainable access

Restrictions should be based on legality, delivery, measurement, consumer protection, fraud, or partner policy. Give specific reasons and an appeal path; do not make subjective judgements about whether an idea deserves a market.

Traceable money and exposure

Keep available funds, restricted sponsorship, pending proceeds, reserves, commitments, and delivered exposure distinct. Record versions of terms, attribution evidence, reversals, and settlements.

Privacy and consent

Use least-privilege integrations, clear consent, protected eligibility evidence, and data minimization. Prefer contextual targeting where practical and provide correction, export, and deletion processes.

No false guarantees

Do not guarantee sales, profit, infinite impressions, or compute earnings. Promise only the defined minimum exposure that can be funded and delivered through authorized inventory.

04 / what the prototype shows

A guided, fictional operations demo

The linked Discovery Desk is a clickable walkthrough of the proposed workflow. It demonstrates how qualification, settled funding, a campaign commitment, sale review, and contributor controls fit together.

The demo is a product conversation aid—not a live advertising, compute, payment, attribution, or seller system.

All demo records are fictional and stored only in the browser session/local interface. No account connection, hardware access, external workload, sale, payment, or campaign launch occurs. A production service would require real provider adapters, operational funding, security and privacy controls, and professional legal, tax, accounting, payments, and regulatory review.
